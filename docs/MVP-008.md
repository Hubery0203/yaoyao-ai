## Owner usage

Settings exposes account nickname, response length, affection, followup/advice policy and timezone. These defaults apply to website and Weixin; a current explicit request takes priority. Concurrent changes require refresh after a version conflict. Nicknames are data, not instructions.

Recent-life settings allows a dated plan or explicit ongoing/completed/cancelled statement and state updates. Automatic capture is deliberately limited to one whole, unquoted first-person statement per turn for 面试/考试/出差/旅行/搬家/体检/开会/项目. Unsupported, compound and ambiguous turns are not silently interpreted. Relative dates use event receipt date and captured timezone. Plans are limited to the recent window (two days past to 31 days ahead); explicit outcomes live for seven days. Expiry means no current-context recall, never a known outcome. Last 100 records are inspectable, at most 12 current entries enter model context. This does not create outreach.

Weixin commands (standalone): `记忆列表`, `记忆帮助`, `记住：我喜欢喝咖啡`, `纠正记忆 XXXXXXXX：我现在不喝咖啡了`, `停用记忆 XXXXXXXX`. The eight-character reference comes from the list. A write previews first; send `确认 ABC123` within ten minutes, or `取消 ABC123`. Only the newest preview remains pending. Supports one explicit semantic preference/name/location at a time, no CORE edits, no permanent deletion. Management replies bypass the model, are durably replayed with their input, and management history cannot seed the normal memory extractor. A disabled slot stays muted against subsequent automatic consolidation; website candidate confirmation or a new explicit confirmed remember can re-enable it. Historical chat and original source remain viewable, so disable is not a claim of universal forgetting.
