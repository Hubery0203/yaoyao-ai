# 010 A/B capability and regression checks

- Short burst retains ordered raw input and one frozen idempotency key; no control merged into ordinary chat.
- Latest topic/share takes priority; a short answer can follow a genuine recent question. No whole burst echo; requested quotation/detail remains intact.
- Explicit rejection stops the rejected suggestion without erasing other topics or facts.
- Commands require actual bound inbound owner; quoted/hypothetical text is ordinary chat.
- Enabling/settings/add requires preview and confirmation; stopping remains immediate and receiving remains available.
- Confirmation scoped to channel/owner, expires, rejects version changes, survives a new store process; source replay does not repeat side effects.
- Wall times use explicit account zone; nonexistent/ambiguous DST or malformed dates rejected. Future window and pending quota rechecked at confirmation.
- Cancelled/queued/sending/accepted statuses reported honestly; no retry of uncertain delivery.
- Existing daily/rolling limits, quiet time, context lifetime, image reservation and migration/RLS checks remain green.
- Mock reports never imply live tone approval. No paid or production action in this development phase.
